# CCEV methodology: structural superiority and replication-barrier falsification
**31 October 2026 — provisional, not endorsement-ready**

## Exact problem and strongest narrow claim
Some published tools begin with provider quotations, invoices, known or average rates, nursery-administered billing profiles, or sourced fee listings. CCEV proposes a different entry point: **the original nursery tariff plus an intended booking, before a complete trusted quote exists**. Multiple conditions must be applied together, with evidence spans and refusal when an essential condition remains unknown. This is a documented-workflow distinction, **not** a claim of exclusive invention or proven whole-product superiority.

## Mathematical contract (proposed)
Let D be a dated document and x a proposed booking. Extract a typed, versioned set of claims R(D) with source spans and precedence. The output decision is:

- ACCEPT with cost C(D,x) only when every decision-critical rule used has a valid source, a compatible scope, non-conflicting precedence and valid units, and all required inputs are known.
- WITHHOLD with the smallest actionable set of unresolved/contradictory premises otherwise.
- A refusal must be correct and specific; simply withholding every difficult case would not demonstrate usefulness.

Rule application must be separated from extraction. Each applied tariff line must identify exact document provenance, temporal applicability and assumptions; no source-backed statement may be silently transformed into a guaranteed family entitlement.

## What has been and has not been demonstrated
1. The existing published [seven-provider comparison](DOCUMENTED_WORKFLOW_COMPARISON_2026-10-31.md) identifies published inputs, procedures, outputs and overlap; it cannot establish undocumented competitor features or matched real outcomes.
2. The reproducible [structural benchmark](structural_benchmark.py) compares two **invented** algorithms: rate-only multiplication versus an explicit source-presence and condition-guard toy method. Its counterexamples demonstrate why a method without a minimum-booking clause lacks information needed to validate bookability. This is a proof of information insufficiency under the stated premises, not evidence that named suppliers fail.
3. Earlier unfamiliar-provider results: 4 of 12 numeric-reference cases matched; 8 withheld. This is not validated automated superiority; absence of bad numerical quotations alone does not show practical coverage.
4. Customer survey opinions are evidence of perceived utility and unmet needs, not independently adjudicated error rates, willingness to pay or verified unique parent customers.

## Proposed future decisive evaluation
- Freeze independent blind original nursery documents with permission/public-source compliance, precise effective-date checks, and an external evaluator's adjudication of all booking-critical clauses and outputs.
- Define a naturalistic task: a parent with D and booking x needs the justified amount, or an explicit decision not to quote with precise missing details.
- Compare: (A) rate-only procedure and its declared input assumptions, (B) parent manually interpreting D, (C) CCEV source verifier. Include actual third-party products only where their documented workflow and terms permit fair testing; label unsupported access as not tested.
- Evaluate exact numeric correctness **and** coverage (never only error-on-quoted cases), unsupported confident quote rate, correct/incorrect withholding, completeness of source evidence, case time, number of user-entered interpreted fees, comprehension and helpfulness.
- Separate: structural proof, software test suite, blind real-document evaluation, direct user task data, and commercial market validation. No metric may stand in for another.

## Replication barrier: credible hypotheses and defeat conditions
| Possible barrier | What must be measured | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Typed clause/evidence graph with conditional precedence and effective dates | End-to-end traces; adversarial conflicts/unknowns; correct exception handling | An ordinary generic extraction+calculator reproduces the necessary accuracy and withholding behavior without major engineering |
| Durable, independently adjudicated tariff-rule corpus | Licensing/provenance, breadth, update costs and blind holdout fidelity | Comparable curated documents and labels can be reproduced quickly and cheaply |
| Calibration of selective decisions (quote vs clarify) | Joint accuracy and answer coverage under shifted providers and new fee templates | Coverage collapses as soon as conditions change, or tuning is trivial for a competitor |
| Efficient consumer workflow | Time-to-action and parent comprehension relative to existing workflows | Parents still need to interpret all charges or cannot act on the output |

Avoid false competitive moats: generic AI prompts, arithmetic, citations and uncertainty notices are readily replicable. Defensibility might emerge from difficult integrated engineering and validated data processes, but must be demonstrated with engineering estimates, repeatable tests and credible comparisons.
