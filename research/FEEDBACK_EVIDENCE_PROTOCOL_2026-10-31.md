# Nursery Fee Clarity — feedback evidence protocol
**Date:** 31 October 2026  
**Use:** Voluntary early-stage research into clarity and usefulness of a **fictional** fee-rule demonstration. Not a longitudinal study, production trial or evidence of actual parental entitlement accuracy.

## Questions (pre-specified)
1. What is the respondent's broad relationship to nursery fees? *Not a verified parent identity.*
2. What approach has the respondent already used (if any) to understand costs?
3. Which cost-related obstacle is most important?
4. Did they try the fictional example, and was it clear?
5. Would the source-referenced explanation and explicit unresolved conditions help?
6. Does the proposed workflow seem better, complementary, equivalent or worse than their current approach?
7. Would they independently choose to try a future tool on **published, non-personal** nursery fee terms?

## Measurement rules
- **Unit:** one public GitHub issue is one feedback submission, **not** one independently verified parent or unique visitor. Possible duplicates and self-selection remain.
- **Denominator:** record the count of *all reviewed valid submissions* and a separate count of those reporting that they tried the demo; do not report a percentage without its denominator.
- **Responses:** retain "did not try", "unsure", "no current method" and "prefer not to say" categories; never recode them to positive.
- **No tracking:** the static preview neither tracks visitors nor counts form opens, clicks or local copies. Conversion rates are therefore **not observable** from this setup.
- **Qualitative:** code recurring problems, concerns and requests; distinguish self-reported preference from actual user performance. Preserve negative feedback.
- **Validity:** no demographic or sensitive data, invoices, children's data, screenshots or provider-specific contracts. Public GitHub posts require sign-in, expose usernames, and create substantial sample-selection bias.
- **Interpretation:** customer preference can support usefulness and demand hypotheses, **not** automatically prove implementation accuracy, innovation, defensibility or endorsement suitability.

## Hypotheses and evidence still required
| Proposition | Current research signal | Additional evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Parents understand source-linked conditional decisions | Self-reported clarity after a fictional demo | Observed comprehension checks and independent participants |
| A document-first workflow reduces manual interpretation | Stated preference relative to usual method | Task-level steps and time recorded against a defined baseline |
| Source rule checking avoids unsupported cost decisions | Logical minimum-booking counterexample | Unseen provider documents with independent rule/total adjudication |
| Users would adopt a future product | Self-reported willingness to try | Actual voluntary follow-up use and sustained engagement |
| CCEV is sufficiently differentiated and defensible | Seven supplier documented-workflow contrasts | Demonstrated method reliability, market positioning, honest replication challenge |

## Collection route
Public GitHub [structured feedback form](https://github.com/Dapson18/nursery-fee-clarity-research/issues/new?template=nursery_research_feedback.yml) plus a separate on-device copy option. A private no-account submission endpoint is **not implemented**. Do not claim that copy-only feedback reaches the research team.

## Privacy and release gates
Before adding an anonymous server form, collecting contact details, allowing real documents, or promoting to families broadly: publish identifiable controller/contact details, lawful basis, data handling/retention policy, spam protections and security review; secure appropriate processing infrastructure. Keep research separate from other projects' storage and private engines.
