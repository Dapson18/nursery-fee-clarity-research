Privacy information

Last updated 31 October 2026.

What this website collects

The static fictional demonstration does not collect names, childcare invoices, children's details, real quotes, or funding information. Choices and calculations are performed in your own browser. No analytics, advertising tags, tracking cookies, or research-signup database are installed here. The host and your network/browser may process technical connection logs.

Local-copy feedback

If you select “Copy research feedback locally”, the site attempts to copy your chosen responses to your device's clipboard. Nothing is sent to our server. You decide separately whether to share it.

Optional GitHub feedback

Following the “Submit structured feedback on GitHub” link opens a third-party GitHub issue form. To submit, you need a GitHub account. Your username, answers, and submission are public and may be indexed or copied. GitHub processes this information according to its own privacy statement. We cannot promise anonymity, confidential storage, or complete removal of copied public posts.

The form asks only for broad, non-identifying multiple-choice opinions about the demonstration. Do not include children's names, addresses, household income, actual contracts, invoices, contact details, medical information or private nursery correspondence. If you do not want your GitHub identity to appear publicly, do not submit that form.

Use and retention

Research responses may be reviewed manually to inform the product concept, using qualitative summaries and counts. Public GitHub issues can remain visible indefinitely unless closed or removed. A named research controller, contact channel, lawful basis and defined retention schedule must be published before any broader public recruitment campaign; the current GitHub form is suitable only as a clearly disclosed voluntary public-feedback route.

Limitations

This research site does not verify real childcare costs or entitlement eligibility. No actual family data is needed. Any future private survey, mailing list, or real document upload workflow would require a revised notice and appropriate data safeguards before activation.
