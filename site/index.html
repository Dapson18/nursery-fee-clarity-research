Early research · England-first

Nursery fees should come with reasons, not just numbers.

A fee schedule can contain hourly rates, minimum bookings, optional extras and funding conditions. We’re researching a clearer way for parents to understand what the document actually supports—and what they still need to ask.

Research preview: This is not a live nursery quote checker, funding eligibility assessment or approved financial/legal advice service. No real nursery prices are calculated on this page.

What we're trying to make clearer

We want to test whether explaining the source and conditions is more useful than showing a single estimated price.

01 · SOURCE

Where did this charge come from?

Connect each number to the particular fee-schedule wording that supports it.

02 · CONDITIONS

Does this rate apply?

Check booking minimums, age bands, session times, funded-hour arrangements and optional charges before presenting a total.

03 · UNCERTAINTY

What is still unknown?

Show questions that need confirmation instead of silently treating unknown information as zero or inventing a definitive fee.

Why this outcome?

  • The two-day minimum is met
  • Breakfast not included
Still requires confirmation

Whether a place is available, whether these published terms are current and whether other contract conditions apply.

All example prices, schedule extracts and decisions on this page are invented solely to illustrate the proposed experience. They are not data from a nursery and do not prove the accuracy of the research system.

Questions you may have

Does this calculate my actual childcare entitlement?

No. Check your eligibility through official government information and your provider. This research page uses an invented example only.

Can I upload my contract or invoice?

Not here. Please do not email personal or child-related documents. The pilot is currently limited to general feedback and public-source research.

How is this different from another nursery fee calculator?

We want to test a document-to-decision workflow that links each fee to supporting wording and explains what cannot be decided. This is an unproven differentiation hypothesis, not a claim that no other service offers something similar.
